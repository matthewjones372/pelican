Pelican · logo mark · directions explored
The four directions, for the record.
Every mark is the same subject: a pelican's bill in profile, upper mandible over pouch. They differ in how the form is constructed — mass, line, letterform, facet. Each is one closed colour and reads at 16px.
A · Silhouette
Mass
Two solid shapes, one hairline of space between them. Heaviest presence in a README header; the safest read at favicon size.
B · Monoline
Line
One constant stroke width throughout — the form as a drawing rather than a shape. Lightest on the page; needs a heavier weight below 20px.
C · Monogram
Letterform
A geometric P whose bowl carries the bill. Says “library” before it says “bird”; the most legible of the four at 16px.
D · Facet
Straight lines only
No curve anywhere — seven vertices total. Reads as something drafted rather than drawn. Sharpest, and the coldest.
Each direction has its own artboard on this canvas — sizes, lockups and inversions.
